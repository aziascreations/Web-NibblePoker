Introduction

Hello there, my name is Herwin Bozet.
I'm a developer who's spent years working in PureBasic, Python, .NET, C, Win32 APIs, embedded systems, and web development.

I like building things that stay out of the way and minimalistic in logic and code.
You'll mainly find CLI tools, small libraries, self-hosted services, and electronics projects for everyday or specific needs.

Finally, everything here, this website included, is open-source under permissive OSI-approved licenses or in the public domain.
It's all built and maintained in my free time, with occasional help from the open-source community.

Core Tenets

Apolitical code

Code does not care about feelings, code does not care about wars, and it certainly does not care about your politics.
Everything I do, and any service I offer, is done in a completely apolitical way.
If you expect to find socially conscious and engaged software, you can look elsewhere.

During the last couple of years, we've seen many developers and companies take stances that either enshittify their product or endanger their users.
This is something that has bothered me to no end, and I refuse to take part in that kind of rhetoric.

Financing

This website, as well as the surrounding infrastructure, was made to cost as little as possible while not being reliant on any censor-happy companies.
Following my core tenets, I also wanted to retain some sort of "technological sovereignty", and therefore refuse to support or use companies that actively fight against legal free speech.

The details of the operating costs are provided below to illustrate the low price of such independence.

And for those of you who are more motivated, I hope to inspire you to at least try this kind of activity.
It may seem daunting at first, but for a fraction of the cost presented here, and with a bit of motivation, you can already accomplish a lot of things and gain valuable knowledge for a world as interconnected as ours.
